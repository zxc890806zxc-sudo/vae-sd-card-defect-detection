# Data policy

`data/samples/` contains selected, small image samples copied from the historical project for presentation. The selected files illustrate the expected kinds of inputs only.

The full training, validation, and test datasets are deliberately excluded. Their ownership, consent, and permission for redistribution have not been verified. Do not use the samples to infer the full data distribution, model performance, or a train/test split.

To reproduce the historical workflows, a future maintainer must obtain authorized datasets and document their source, license, split, preprocessing, and any applicable confidentiality restrictions.
